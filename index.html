Unelected Power

Utah government runs on more than the officials voters put on the ballot. This series tracks people who hold appointed positions — who appointed them, when, and, where it applies, how they got there from an elected seat. One profile per person, built exclusively from primary documents.

Governor's Office · State Housing Coordinator

Steve Waldrip

Two terms in the Utah House, then appointed Gov. Cox's Senior Advisor for Housing Strategy in Dec. 2023 — no vote required. A 2026 law made him state housing coordinator over a new division, and repealed the housing-affordability board he used to sit on.

MIDA Board · CEO, Northern Wasatch Realtors

Mike Ostermiller

Never elected to anything. A seat on the Military Installation Development Authority's board since its 2008 founding year, a lobbying practice running 14 years straight, and a role in the 2020 Hideout annexation bill — all without ever appearing on a ballot.

Runs the statewide Realtors' association — and, through the same firm as Ostermiller, is also that association's own registered lobbyist, plus roughly 30 other paying clients including a state land agency. Never elected to anything.
