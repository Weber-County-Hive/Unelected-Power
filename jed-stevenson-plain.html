What this is

Jed Stevenson has never run for office and holds no government appointment.

He runs two private companies that work for Utah charter schools. One builds the schools. The other handles their back-office work, like IT, staff paperwork, bookkeeping and buildings.

Who's involved

  • Jed Stevenson: founded One West Construction in 2005; president of Academica West.
  • Sheldon Killpack: vice president of Academica West and a manager of One West Construction. He was state Senate majority leader and resigned in 2010.
  • Jerry Stevenson: state senator for Senate District 6 since 2010, retiring. The Salt Lake Tribune reported in 2016 that he is Jed's father.
  • Academica West and Eminent Technical Solutions: the companies that handle back-office work for charter schools.
  • Career Path High (Kaysville) and Bridge Elementary (Roy): charter schools named in board records.

What the records show

The builder: One West Construction LLC was formed Nov. 1, 2005, in Kaysville. Its state filing lists two managers: Jed Stevenson and Sheldon Killpack. Its own client list shows more than 30 charter-school projects in Utah and Idaho since 2006, including HighMark Charter School (South Weber), North Davis Preparatory Academy and Leadership Learning Academy (Layton), and Jefferson Academy (Kaysville).

The manager: A 2016 Salt Lake Tribune investigation reported that Academica West and its sister company, Eminent Technical Solutions, worked as the IT, HR, bookkeeping and facilities departments for 17 Utah charter schools. They charged a fee per student ($400 for a school's first 500 students, $350 after) and took in at least $6.9 million from charter schools in one year. Academica West's own revenue grew from about $3 million (2013-14) to $4.7 million in 2015.

The Senate seat: Sheldon Killpack resigned from the Senate in 2010 after a DUI. Jerry Stevenson was appointed to fill that seat, Senate District 6, and has held it for sixteen years. He is retiring this cycle. He gave $500 to Tamara Tran, who is taking the seat, on 6/22/26. Jed D. Stevenson gave her campaign $4,000 the same day.

School boards: Career Path High's 2022 board roster shows Jerry Stevenson as board president from 9/12/14 to 6/30/23, and lists Academica West itself on a board committee. The board met at One West Construction's Kaysville office. Those ties ended in 2023. In Roy, Bridge Elementary's board considered on Sept. 19, 2023 adding Jed Stevenson and Killpack as signers on the school's bank accounts. The motion was tabled, not adopted.

Other businesses: Jed Stevenson, Killpack and Fernando J. Zulueta are members of Bluffdale School Development LLC. Jerry Stevenson's own disclosure form lists a 33% interest in Shed Tremonton LLC, which he co-manages with Killpack.

What isn't known yet

  • Which Bluffdale charter school Bluffdale School Development LLC was formed for.
  • Who the third family member co-managing Shed Tremonton LLC is.
  • Whether Bob Stevenson, the HD-16 candidate, is related to Jerry Stevenson.
  • Whether "Kate Stevenson," a Career Path High secretary, is related to any of them. It is a shared last name only.
