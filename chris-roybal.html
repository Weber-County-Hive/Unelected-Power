President of an economic alliance, manager of a PAC

Chris Roybal is president of the Northern Utah Economic Alliance (NUEA), a role he has held since July 2019. His own LinkedIn profile describes 25 years of experience in economic development, consulting, and executive management, but his listed work history shows an intervening decade outside that field. His own "About" summary separately states he served as CEO of the Economic Development Corporation of Utah (EDCUtah) — the same organization Ryan Starks now leads — though that role doesn't appear as a separately dated entry in his listed work history.

NUEA is itself an incorporated Utah entity (formed July 16, 2020), and Roybal holds three roles on its own state filing: agent, director, and officer. He is separately listed as a member of Utah Forward PAC, LLC (formed Aug. 15, 2022), a political action committee — one member among others, not its controlling officer. Roybal and his wife, Cindy Roybal, are also listed among the PAC's own 2022 donors, at $1,000. He is also president of the Wasatch Avian Education Society, a bird-education nonprofit unrelated to his economic-development work.

Utah Forward PAC gave $4,927.20 to Keep Our Valley Free — the organized opposition to Ogden Valley's 2024 incorporation vote, which commissioned a competing Utah Foundation feasibility study warning the new city could face funding shortfalls. Keep Our Valley Free's own PIC registration lists Laura Warburton as Director and CFO/Treasurer — the same person, at the same Huntsville home address, listed as Utah Forward PAC's own Manager. The address is confirmed in multiple places: Keep Our Valley Free's PIC registration and Utah Forward PAC's filings (disclosures.utah.gov) and Utah Forward PAC LLC's state business record (search "Utah Forward PAC"). One of the PAC's 2022 donors, Atlee Wampler ($5,000), is the father of Janet Wampler, who was elected the new city's first mayor in November 2025. His gift was made in April 2022, before the PAC's payments to Keep Our Valley Free; the PAC's filings do not tie any one donor's money to any one payment. Bruce Warburton, likely her husband, is listed as a second officer at the same address.

Payments to Commissioner Froerer

Utah Forward PAC paid Weber County Commissioner Gage Froerer $5,000 on 7/11/2022 and $5,000 on 5/4/2023, both listed with the purpose "Political Support" — $10,000 total. Froerer separately serves as Weber County's Co-Chair on NUEA's Advisory Board, the organization Roybal leads.

Froerer also holds a gubernatorial appointment to the Military Installation Development Authority (MIDA) board, drawn from among Weber County's sitting commissioners. The Salt Lake Tribune (May 6, 2026) lists him as a current voting member; MIDA board members are unpaid, per the same report. MIDA's confirmed Weber County project, the Falcon Hill Aerospace Research Park at Hill Air Force Base, is separate from the West Weber Inland Port project area referenced below.

Donor context: West Weber

Several of Utah Forward PAC's donors have documented business interests in the West Weber area, where infrastructure financing (including a sewer lift station, a $5 million UIPA loan, and a public infrastructure district bond) has been approved by Weber County in recent years. Nilson & Company operates active residential developments in Weber County, including Orchards at JDC Ranch and The Grove at JDC Ranch in Plain City. Rincon Financial is a corporate vehicle for developer Shane Dunleavy. R&O Construction separately paid Froerer $1,000 directly in 2018, in addition to its $5,000 to Utah Forward PAC in 2022.

Northern Wasatch Association of Realtors (NWAR) — a separate organization from NUEA — paid Froerer $5,000 four times between 2018 and 2022, for $20,000 total. NWAR's CEO throughout that period, Mike Ostermiller, has served on MIDA's board since 2008.

Roybal has also served as a public proponent of the West Weber development itself, appearing alongside Froerer and Weber County Economic Development Director Stephanie Sage Russell to promote it — including as a potential Utah Inland Port Authority satellite site. Russell herself previously worked at NUEA before joining the county.

The Utah Inland Port Authority's draft minutes for its May 20, 2024 board meeting, where the board adopted the West Weber County Inland Port Project Area Plan, list Roybal among the people in attendance; Russell presented the county's plan at that meeting. Neither Roybal nor Froerer is listed among the board members present.

Speaker Mike Schultz did sit on the UIPA board. UIPA's approved minutes list him as a voting board member in October 2022 and October 2023, when he moved and seconded motions. UIPA announced on Dec. 8, 2023 that he was leaving the board, effective immediately. That means he was on the board in July and August 2023, when Weber County first proposed a West Weber project area and asked UIPA to adopt one. He was gone 10 days before UIPA staff asked the county, on Dec. 18, 2023, to expand the area to about 9,000 acres, and before the board adopted the West Weber plan in May 2024. The UIPA minutes we read do not show him voting on West Weber.

Open questions

What is Building Construction Partners LLC's interest in West Weber?

Building Construction Partners LLC gave Utah Forward PAC $10,000 in 2022. The entity (formed 2013, registered in Lehi, Utah County) does not have a named project or established presence in Weber County identified so far.

How did Commissioner Froerer vote on the specific West Weber milestones?

This file documents the financial relationship between Utah Forward PAC and Froerer. Froerer's individual recorded votes on West Weber's 2023-2024 milestones (the original project proposal, a later expansion resolution, and UIPA adoption) have not yet been confirmed against meeting minutes.

Are there other members or owners of Utah Forward PAC LLC besides Roybal?

Utah Forward PAC, LLC's registered agent is Katrina Gibson and its manager is Laura Warburton — Roybal is a member, not the entity's controlling officer.

Did Warburton disclose her dual role managing both the giving PAC and the receiving committee?

Laura Warburton is Utah Forward PAC's own Manager and, per Keep Our Valley Free's PIC registration, also that committee's Director and CFO/Treasurer — both at her home address. Whether this dual role was disclosed anywhere at the time of the $4,927.20 in payments, or required to be, has not been confirmed.

What is "Arena," and why did it receive the PAC's single largest payment?

Utah Forward PAC paid $17,798 to an entity or vendor called "Arena" on July 20, 2022, listed with the purpose "Political Support" — the largest single payment in the PAC's confirmed spending. A vendor by a similar name has separately appeared in research on another Weber County campaign's vendor payments. Whether these are the same "Arena," and what services were provided for this amount, has not been confirmed.

When did Roybal serve as EDCUtah's CEO, and can it be independently confirmed?

Roybal's own LinkedIn "About" summary states he served as CEO of EDCUtah, but this role does not appear as a separately dated entry in his listed work history there, which otherwise runs continuously from 2005 to the present across other positions. Independent confirmation (e.g., from EDCUtah's own historical materials or contemporaneous news coverage) has not been found.
