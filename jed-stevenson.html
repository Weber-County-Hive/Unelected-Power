Case File · Never elected · never appointed to public office · UP 300.01

Jed Stevenson

Posted Sept. 22, 2026 · Updated Oct. 27, 2026
Founder, One West Construction (2005) · President, Academica West

Jed Stevenson has never run for office and holds no government appointment. He doesn't need one. Through one construction company and one management firm, he builds the buildings, runs the finances, and has sat on the governing boards of a meaningful share of northern Utah's charter schools — while his father held a state Senate seat for sixteen years, and his business partner is the senator his father replaced.

The short version

  • Jed Stevenson has never been elected or appointed to public office. He founded One West Construction LLC in 2005, which lists more than 30 charter-school projects, and he is president of Academica West, a charter-management company.
  • A 2016 Salt Lake Tribune investigation reported that Academica West and its sister company, Eminent Technical Solutions, served 17 Utah charter schools and took in at least $6.9 million from charter schools in a single year.
  • The same Tribune story reported that Jed Stevenson is the son of Sen. Jerry Stevenson. His business partner, Sheldon Killpack, is the senator whose 2010 resignation opened the Senate District 6 seat Jerry Stevenson was appointed to fill.
  • Career Path High's 2022 board roster lists Jerry Stevenson as board president (9/12/14 to 6/30/23), with meetings at One West Construction's Kaysville address. In September 2023, Bridge Elementary's board considered adding Jed Stevenson and Killpack as bank signers; the motion was tabled.

Two companies, one name behind both

One West Construction LLC (entity #6035468-0160, Utah Division of Corporations) was formed November 1, 2005 and is based at 290 N. Flint St., Kaysville. Its state filing lists two Managers: Jed Stevenson and Sheldon Killpack. One West's own published client list shows more than 30 charter-school construction projects across Utah and Idaho since 2006, including several inside the Layton/South Weber area this outlet already tracks — HighMark Charter School (South Weber), North Davis Preparatory Academy and Leadership Learning Academy (Layton), Jefferson Academy (Kaysville) — plus two rounds of construction on Academica West's own Kaysville office (2008, 2014).

Academica West is the charter-management company Jed Stevenson leads as president, with Sheldon Killpack as vice president. Per a 2016 Salt Lake Tribune investigation, Academica West and its sister company Eminent Technical Solutions functioned as the outsourced IT, HR, bookkeeping, and facilities departments for 17 Utah charter schools, billing a per-student fee (was $400 for a school's first 500 students, $350 after) that took in at least $6.9 million from charter schools in a single year. Academica West's own revenue climbed from roughly $3 million (2013-14) to $4.7 million in 2015.

Where the governance access has reached

Career Path High · historical, not current confirmed, 2014-2023

Career Path High's own 2022 board roster shows Jerry Stevenson served as Board President from 9/12/14 through 6/30/23, and Clint Morris served as Financial Coordinator (through 10/31/23) — a role with Audit Committee duties alongside the Board Chair. The same roster lists "Academica West" itself as a member of the Board Accreditation Standards Committee, directly inside governance rather than as an outside contractor. Board meetings were held at 290 N. Flint St., Kaysville — One West Construction's address. Career Path High's current board page (checked 9/22/26) lists neither Jerry Stevenson nor anyone matching that history, and the school's listed address has since changed to 550 E 300 S, Kaysville. These ties were real, and were officer-level, but ended in 2023.

Bridge Elementary, Roy · bank signing authority confirmed

Board minutes from September 19, 2023 show Bridge Elementary's board considered adding both Jed Stevenson and Sheldon Killpack as signers on the school's operating bank accounts — beyond petty cash, direct access to the school's day-to-day funds. The motion was tabled, not adopted.

Bluffdale School Development LLC open lead

Utah business entity #5854029-0160. Members: Jed Stevenson, Sheldon Killpack, and Fernando J. Zulueta (a name tied to Academica's national charter-management network outside Utah). Which specific Bluffdale-area charter school this entity was formed to develop has not yet been identified.

Shed Tremonton LLC — a second Jerry Stevenson/Killpack business tie confirmed

Jerry Stevenson's own Conflict of Interest disclosure form lists a 33% interest in Shed Tremonton LLC. That entity's co-managers are Jerry Stevenson and Sheldon Killpack — the same business partner Jed Stevenson shares at both Academica West and One West Construction, and the senator whose 2010 resignation created the Senate seat Jerry Stevenson filled — plus a third, unnamed family member not yet identified from the records reviewed so far. This is a second, separate business relationship between Jerry Stevenson and Killpack, outside the charter-school companies Jed runs, and it means the Killpack tie runs through both generations of the family, not just Jed's.
