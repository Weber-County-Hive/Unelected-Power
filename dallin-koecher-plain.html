What this is

Dallin Koecher has never run for office. Since March 2020 he has been Executive Director of the Heber Valley Chamber, Tourism, and Economic Development office, also called the Heber Valley CVB. Before that he was the office's Communications Manager for six years.

He also sits on the Wasatch Open Lands Board. That county board looks at requests to protect land and suggests how the county's Open Space bond money is spent.

The form

Members of that board must fill out a conflict-of-interest form every year. State law (Utah Code 17-70-505 through -507) requires it. The form asks members to list companies or groups where they are an officer, director, agent, employee or owner of 10% or more.

Koecher signed his form on Feb. 4, 2026, and it was notarized. He listed one thing: "Heber Valley Chamber of Commerce — Director." That is a short way to name his real job, so it is accurate as far as it goes.

His form is one of only three, out of seven board members' forms, posted on the county's board page.

What the records show

State business filings, OpenCorporates and Heber City payroll records list five more positions that are not on his form:

1. CAMS, director since 2019. CAMS has a $39,900-a-year events contract with Heber City.
2. Heber Valley Music, director and trustee since 2019.
3. Wasatch County Arts Council, incorporator and director since 2016.
4. Red White & Blue Festival Inc., director.
5. Heber City Board of Adjustments, 2019–2021, per payroll records.

CAMS, the Red White & Blue Festival and the Arts Council all share one Main Street address in Heber City. Heber City Manager Matt Brower is also a CAMS director. Rachel Kahler, CAMS's paid director, sits on the Red White & Blue Festival board with Koecher. The Chamber/CVB's own Executive Board is chaired by Mark Nelson, a Wasatch County Councilman.

What isn't known yet

Payroll records list Koecher's employer as "Wasatch County." It is not settled whether the Chamber/CVB is a county department or an independent nonprofit. The full case file does not answer that.

Koecher was not asked for comment. The Hive reports from public records only.
